# Communication & Escalation Paths

Primary owner for crisis communications: **Rosemary Achieng (PR/Crisis Manager)**.

| Channel | Audience | Owner | Escalation Stage 1 | Escalation Stage 2 | Escalation Stage 3 | Escalation Stage 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dashboard Banner | Public users | Rosemary Achieng | Publish initial notice | Add recurring status blocks | Add executive notice and pinned FAQ | Maintain final resolution + archive link |
| Email Distribution Lists | Partners, agencies, enterprise stakeholders | Rosemary Achieng + Incident Commander | Send incident detected template | Send investigation updates every declared interval | Include legal-approved language + direct contacts | Send resolution, lessons, and review schedule |
| SMS/Phone Tree | Critical stakeholders and emergency contacts | Duty Comms Officer (on behalf of Rosemary) | Priority alert to predefined list | Escalate to backup contacts if no acknowledgement | Escalate to executive incident bridge | Send closure confirmation |
| Incident Bridge (call/video) | Internal response leads | Incident Commander | Open bridge and assign roles | Invite legal/compliance (Kimberly) when needed | Invite executive leadership for critical severity | Transition to post-incident review |
| Regulator/Government Channel | Regulators, government focal points | Compliance + Rosemary Achieng | Notify per required reporting window | Provide verified updates only | Issue legal-reviewed interim statements | File final closure and audit record |

## Escalation Triggers
- **Stage 1:** Incident detected and verified.
- **Stage 2:** Ongoing impact or missing acknowledgements.
- **Stage 3:** Critical severity, regulatory exposure, or broad service disruption.
- **Stage 4:** Resolution and post-incident reporting.
