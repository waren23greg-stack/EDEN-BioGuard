# EDEN-BioGuard User Consent & Data Processing Notice (Draft)

> **Prepared for legal review by Kimberly Onduru.**
> This document is an operational draft and **not legal advice**.

## 1) Purpose of This Consent

EDEN-BioGuard requests your consent to process software and ecological data in order to:
- detect and respond to system misuse or security incidents;
- detect biodiversity/ecosystem risk signals;
- support lawful public-interest alerts and compliance reporting;
- maintain auditable records for accountability and safety review.

## 2) Data Categories Processed

### A. Software/System Data
- account identifiers (minimum necessary);
- authentication and access logs;
- device and application telemetry;
- incident/alert acknowledgement records.

### B. Ecological & Field Data
- biodiversity observations and incident metadata;
- geospatial data (including potentially land-linked coordinates);
- timestamped field reports and validation notes;
- aggregated environmental risk indicators for dashboards/alerts.

## 3) Legal-Context Principles Applied (Kenya)

EDEN-BioGuard applies the following principles during collection, processing, storage, and disclosure:
- **Privacy (Constitution Art. 31):** collect only what is necessary; avoid unjustified personal intrusion.
- **Property and land-related rights (Constitution Art. 40):** avoid harmful misuse of land-linked information and support fair treatment in land-impact decisions.
- **Public accountability (Constitution Art. 10):** ensure transparency, traceability, and responsible governance.
- **Data Protection Act, 2019 principles:** lawful and fair processing, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, retention control, and data subject safeguards.

## 4) Public Alerts and Data Sharing

EDEN-BioGuard may issue public or stakeholder alerts where ecosystem or safety risks justify disclosure. In such cases, EDEN-BioGuard will:
- prefer area-level/aggregated disclosure over personal data publication;
- remove or mask direct personal identifiers where feasible;
- document the legal and operational rationale for publication;
- preserve auditable records of what was shared, when, and why.

## 5) User Rights and Choices

Where applicable under Kenyan law and project governance, users may:
- request access to their personal data records;
- request correction of inaccurate personal data;
- request deletion or restriction where legally permissible;
- withdraw consent for optional processing activities.

**Important:** Withdrawal does not automatically invalidate processing already undertaken under lawful obligations. In the Kenyan context, this may include mandatory incident/accountability record retention, audit-trail preservation, and other statutory/regulatory compliance records where deletion is not immediately permissible.

## 6) Retention & Security Commitments

EDEN-BioGuard will:
- retain personal data only for defined operational/legal periods;
- implement role-based access controls for sensitive software/ecological records;
- maintain audit logs for consent changes and data access events;
- review retention and access controls periodically.

## 7) Consent Statement (UI/Workflow Ready)

"I understand that EDEN-BioGuard will process my software and ecological data for biodiversity protection, security response, and lawful public-interest alerts. I understand my rights and the privacy safeguards described in this notice."

**Required Acceptance Control:**
- `[ ] I consent to EDEN-BioGuard data processing as described above.`
