# EDEN-BioGuard Blockchain & BioImpact Token Policy Guidance (Draft)

> **Prepared for legal review by Kimberly Onduru.**
> This guidance supports implementation planning and is **not legal advice**.

## 1) Policy Scope

This document sets compliance-oriented guardrails for any EDEN-BioGuard blockchain features, including the BioImpact Token, with focus on:
- lawful and fair ecosystem incentive design;
- land/community compensation fairness;
- misuse prevention and traceable auditability;
- Kenyan constitutional and data-protection alignment.

## 2) Compliance Notes (Kenya Context)

- Apply constitutional values of **human dignity, equity, and accountability** (Art. 10) in token governance.
- Respect **privacy rights** (Art. 31) when publishing on-chain/off-chain linked records.
- Respect **property/land rights** (Art. 40) in compensation logic and land-impact decisions.
- Ensure personal data is not irreversibly exposed on-chain in identifiable form.
- Keep sensitive data off-chain and store only hashed references/commitments where possible.

## 3) Fair Land Compensation Principles

For any token-linked land/ecosystem incentive mechanism:

1. **Lawful entitlement mapping:** compensation criteria must be grounded in verified land/community rights documentation.
2. **No discriminatory allocation:** allocation rules must be transparent and consistently applied.
3. **Impact-linked fairness:** payouts should map to measurable ecological stewardship outcomes and verified participation.
4. **Dispute pathway:** users/communities must have a documented challenge and appeals process.
5. **Human oversight:** final high-impact determinations should include qualified human review, not automated-only decisions.

## 4) BioImpact Token Usage Restrictions

BioImpact Token must **not** be positioned as:
- a guaranteed-return investment product;
- a substitute for statutory land title determination;
- a mechanism to bypass local authority/regulatory controls;
- compensation where legal ownership/community rights have not been verified.

Additional controls:
- prohibit token issuance from unverified ecological claims;
- require dual-approval for high-value disbursements;
- block sanctioned or policy-restricted addresses/users where required.

## 5) Auditability & Control Requirements

- Maintain immutable transaction records with clear event metadata.
- Maintain off-chain case files linking token events to source validation evidence.
- Version governance rules and preserve change history.
- Record reviewer identity, approval timestamps, and rationale for exceptions.
- Provide periodic reconciliation reports (on-chain totals vs approved off-chain records).

## 6) Data Handling for Public Alerts & Token Events

When token events intersect with public environmental alerts:
- release only information necessary for public safety and trust;
- avoid publishing personally identifiable beneficiary data;
- use aggregated or pseudonymized summaries where possible;
- document the proportionality rationale for each disclosure.

## 7) Minimum Governance Workflow

1. Validate ecological claim and land/community entitlement evidence.
2. Run policy and compliance checks (privacy, fairness, eligibility).
3. Approve or reject through dual-review.
4. Execute token event and archive evidence.
5. Publish transparency summary without exposing personal data.
6. Trigger periodic legal/compliance review.
